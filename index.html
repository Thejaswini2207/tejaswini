The Process

Four centuries of unbroken craft

The making of a Mysuru silk saree is a ritual — unhurried, precise, and passed from hand to hand across generations.

I
Silk Reeling

Mulberry cocoons from Karnataka farms are hand-reeled to yield threads of exceptional lustre and strength.

II
Dyeing

Yarns are degummed for the legendary Mysuru sheen, then dyed in vivid colourfast pigments — some recipes unchanged for generations.

III
Zari Warping

Gold and silver zari threads, alloyed with real precious metals, are measured and warped into the loom alongside dyed silk.

IV
Handloom Weaving

The weaver interlaces every thread by sight and memory. A single saree takes three to seven days to complete.
